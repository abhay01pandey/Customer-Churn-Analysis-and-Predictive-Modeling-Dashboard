# Overview-
Analyzed customer churn data (27% rate, 1,732 lost customers) using MySQL to structure and clean datasets, identify key churn drivers, and build an interactive Power BI. The analysis revealed that short-term contracts and poor service attitude were major churn factors, enabling targeted retention strategies that improved customer retention and reduced revenue loss.

# Key Features-
KPIs: Highlights 1,732 churned customers (27% churn rate) out of 6,418 total, giving a clear picture of the business impact.

Contract Risk Analysis: Month-to-Month customers show the highest churn, marking them as the most vulnerable group.

Geographic Hotspots: States like Jammu and Assam show above-average churn, helping target localized retention efforts.

Root Cause Breakdown: Identifies churn due to poor service attitude and competitive offers with better pricing or devices.

Service Impact Analysis: Reveals high churn among Fiber Optic users, guiding product and service quality improvements.

# Key Insights-
1. Short-Term Contracts: Customers on Month-to-Month plans are least loyal — shift them to long-term contracts.
2. Customer Service Issues: Poor service attitude is the main churn driver — requires urgent staff retraining.
3. Competitive Pressure: Competitors better offers and speeds demand pricing and feature benchmarking. 
4. Targeted Retention: Focus efforts on high-risk regions and Fiber Optic users to optimize marketing spend.

# Tools & Technologies-
Database & Query Language: MySQL

Data Analysis & Modeling: SQL Views

Visualization & Dashboarding: Power Bl

Spreadsheet Software: Microsoft Excel

# Real-World Application- 
Service Quality: Implement staff retraining to fix service attitude issues.

Targeted Marketing: Focus retention campaigns on risky customer segments and regions.

Product Strategy: Invest in better devices, faster speeds, and improved network stability to stay competitive.
